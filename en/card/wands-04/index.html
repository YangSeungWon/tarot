Four of Wands

Suit of Wands

  • Celebration
  • Stability
  • Belonging

Upright

You finish a stage and take joy in it together. Home, family and belonging grow solid.

Reversed

The celebration is delayed, or belonging is unsteady. It may be harmony only on the surface.

By topic

LoveWhether you can settle somewhere together is the question.
WorkThe steadiness of a place after one stage closes.
MoneyMoney goes to home and living. Look at whether it becomes a foundation.
PeopleEase inside the group you belong to is the issue.
