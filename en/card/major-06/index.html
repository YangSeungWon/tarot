The Lovers

Major Arcana 6

  • Choice
  • Union
  • Values
  • Attraction

Upright

A meeting where minds match, or a fork where you must name what you love more. Choose by values, not by arithmetic.

Reversed

A mismatched pairing, a decision postponed. Wanting both is muddying it. Separate temptation from what is real.

By topic

LoveWhat you value more is the question. Look at values rather than conditions.
WorkIt is a choice of who to work with and which road. Try not to hold both.
MoneySplit or commit is the core. Picking one is usually easier.
PeopleThis is the point where the right people and the wrong ones separate.
