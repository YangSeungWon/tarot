Upright

An old bond or a familiar warmth comes round. A season of plain goodwill given and taken.

LoveOld people and old memories.
WorkPast work or former colleagues coming round again.
MoneyThe value in something old is the core.
PeopleDistance with a long-standing friend.

LoveYou are holding what cannot be returned to. Look at now.
