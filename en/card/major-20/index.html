Judgement

Major Arcana 20

  • Awakening
  • Reckoning
  • Calling
  • Forgiveness

Upright

You settle what has passed and are called into a new phase. Time to face an old problem directly. Forgiveness, of yourself above all, is the key.

Reversed

You are pretending not to hear the call. Self-blame has your ankle, or you keep deferring the verdict.

By topic
