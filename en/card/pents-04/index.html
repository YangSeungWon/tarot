Four of Pentacles

Suit of Pentacles

  • Keeping
  • Tightness
  • Control

Upright

You hold what you have and guard it. Stable, and equally closed to anything new.

Reversed

You loosen the grip, or let it run out carelessly. Check where the money moves.

By topic

LoveThe wish to keep hold. Watch whether it stifles the other person.
WorkWhether guarding your place blocks the new.
MoneyBetween holding and putting to work.
PeopleThe habit of not offering yourself.
