Wheel of Fortune

Major Arcana 10

  • Turning
  • Flow
  • Opportunity
  • Cycle

Upright

The situation moves on its own. Change arrives regardless of effort, so ride it. Long-stuck things loosen now.

Reversed

The turn is unfavourable, or the same pattern repeats. Rather than spending force on what you cannot control, prepare to hold on.
