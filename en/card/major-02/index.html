The High Priestess

Major Arcana 2

  • Intuition
  • Secrets
  • Inner voice
  • Waiting

Upright

The answer is inside rather than outside. Something is still hidden, so watch quietly instead of rushing. Instinct is unusually accurate now.

Reversed

You are ignoring your own voice, or confusing what should stay private with what should be shared. Something concealed may surface soon.

By topic

LoveWhat has not been said yet is the heart of it. Watching quietly, without rushing, helps.
WorkThe question is whether facts are still hidden. Holding off on a conclusion helps.
MoneyUncertain information is the issue. Stay back until you can verify the basis.
PeopleThe subject is the part the other person is not showing.
