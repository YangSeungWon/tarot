Seven of Pentacles

Suit of Pentacles

  • Patience
  • Review
  • Investment

Upright

What you planted is growing. Pull it now and it is early. Waiting is also labour.

LoveEarly or ripe. Leaving it a little longer helps.
WorkThe middle point of something still growing.
MoneyThe stretch where nothing seems to show.
PeopleWhether the time you put in has ripened.

Reversed

Nothing shows and you are restless. If the direction is wrong, turning the soil is an answer too.

LoveNothing shows and you are restless. Check the direction.
WorkTurning the soil may be the answer.
MoneyIt is not returning in proportion to the wait.
PeopleYou are holding on because of the time you put in.
