The Star

Major Arcana 17

  • Hope
  • Recovery

Upright

Quiet light falls where the storm passed. You heal without hurrying, and sincerity carries. It is alright to believe again.

Reversed

Hope is gone and cynicism moved in. Faith in yourself is shaky. Build again from something small.
