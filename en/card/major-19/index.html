The Sun

Major Arcana 19

  • Success
  • Vitality
  • Clarity
  • Joy

Upright

Bright, with nothing to hide. Work goes well and bonds turn transparent. You are recognised as you actually are.

LoveWhether this is a bond with nothing to hide.
WorkA phase of showing and being recognised. Say what is worth saying.
MoneyEarning and spending grow together. Try not to get carried away.
PeopleWho leaves you with more energy is the theme.

Reversed

The joy is dimmed or delayed. You cannot enjoy what is going well, or optimism is covering the facts.

LoveIt is going well and you cannot enjoy it. Look at what is covering it.
WorkThe result is hidden or delayed.
MoneyOptimism may be covering the facts. Look at the numbers.
PeopleWhy the company does not lift you is the theme.
