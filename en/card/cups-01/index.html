Suit of Cups

  • New feeling
  • Opening

Upright

The heart opens. This is the starting point where a new bond or feeling flows in.

LoveWhether to open is the question. Not weighing it helps.
WorkWhether it is work you can care about.
MoneyMoney spent where the heart goes is the core.
PeopleWhether there is someone to share it with.

Reversed

You are keeping feeling shut. Something unexpressed has pooled.

LoveYou are keeping it shut. Check what has pooled unexpressed.
WorkYou cannot attach to it.
MoneyThere is no urge to spend.
PeopleThis is the place of feeling there is nobody to share with.
