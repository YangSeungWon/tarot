The Empress

Major Arcana 3

  • Abundance
  • Care
  • Creation
  • Senses

Upright

What you nurtured bears fruit. There is room now to hold and tend, in work or in a bond. Enjoy the body and the beauty around you.

LoveHolding and having room is the theme. Look after your body and your mood together.
WorkThe question is whether what you grew has ripened.
MoneyCheck whether what comes in and what goes out are rising together.
PeopleThe heart of it is who is caring for whom.

Reversed

Either smothering or neglect. You may be emptying yourself caring for others. If creating feels blocked, rest is the answer.

LoveHolding and smothering divide here. Check that the other person has room to breathe.
WorkWhat you were growing is not ripening. Rest may be the answer.
MoneyCheck whether your spending hand has loosened.
PeopleWhether caring for others is emptying you is the question.
